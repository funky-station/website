a note from tay

hi everyone! correspond and i had a great time putting this together for the funky station community, and hopefully the wider space station community! this is our first time doing a mass pre-order campaign, so we're hoping it goes well (and everything arrives before Funkmas).

this will be my third Funkmas with funky station and i am very proud to call myself the host of funky station. funky station has been a very big part of my life for the past 3 years and i have met many creative and talented people. a big part of the reason why i wanted to do this was to give the community a chance to stand out. i firmly believe that funky station can be a platform to show off talent that i personally think needs to be discovered asap.

depending on how well this goes, we may do more physical releases of funky station specific merch, and i personally would love to crowdfund and help create a new trailer for the game. one centered around characters and the games dynamics, rather than just gameplay. (this isnt a dig at wizden either, i like their trailer too!)

i hope you enjoy this cd, and the effort of all the artists put into it. heres to hoping we hit our goal!

- taydeo, funky station host
